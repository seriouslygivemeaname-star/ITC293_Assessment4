About Us

AerisAg is an agricultural technology and spatial mapping business developed for this electronic work portfolio. The business brings together practical land management knowledge, drone mapping, GIS analysis and web-based communication.

AerisAg brings together more than 10 years of practical hands-on experience in horticulture and agriculture with developing skills in GIS, spatial analysis, database management and information technology. This combination of field-based knowledge and technical skill allows AerisAg to deliver mapping and spatial services that are grounded in real-world land management, vegetation and site conditions.

Our Aim

Our aim is to provide clients with clear, reliable and practical spatial information that supports better decision-making. Through drone mapping, GIS analysis and structured data management, we help councils, rural industries, landholders and design professionals better understand land, assets, vegetation and site conditions for planning, monitoring and management.

Our Values

Our values are built around safety, professionalism and strong data security. We are committed to conducting drone operations responsibly, with careful planning and risk awareness, while also ensuring that project imagery and spatial data are handled with confidentiality and kept under company control.

AerisAg also recognises that land can have cultural and community meaning. Mapping and spatial data should be handled respectfully, especially where land information may relate to Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples, connection to Country, privacy or data sovereignty.

How AerisAg Works

  1. Discuss the client need, site context and mapping purpose.
  2. Capture or source suitable imagery and spatial data.
  3. Process the data using mapping and analysis workflows.
  4. Identify issues, patterns and priority locations.
  5. Deliver practical maps, files or reports to support action and planning.

Why It Matters

Using drones, satellite imagery and digital analysis can save time, improve coverage and support more efficient and sustainable land management practices.

The value of AerisAg is not only in collecting imagery, but in turning spatial information into useful outputs that can support practical decisions.

Better data leads to better decisions.
