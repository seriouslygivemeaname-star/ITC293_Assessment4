What Do We Do?

AerisAg delivers drone-based mapping, aerial imagery, GIS analysis and site reporting for councils, land managers, rural properties, architects and landscape professionals. Our workflows are designed to provide practical, decision-ready outputs rather than just imagery.

This Assessment 4 website also acts as an electronic work portfolio. It demonstrates skills in web development, drone technology, GIS mapping and land management through the AerisAg business concept.

Main Services Offered

Property Aerial Mapping

Vegetation Monitoring

  • Crop condition maps.

Who We Work With

We support councils and public sector teams with aerial mapping, site inspections, vegetation monitoring, drainage assessment and spatial reporting for land and asset management.

We work with farms, rural properties, horticultural businesses and land management projects, providing drone mapping and site intelligence to better understand vegetation, drainage, access and land condition.

We also assist architects, landscape designers, consultants and other professionals with orthomosaics, point clouds, 3D site capture and existing-condition mapping for planning and design.

What Sets Us Apart

  • GIS-backed delivery - We combine drone capture with mapping and spatial analysis to produce practical, decision-ready outputs.
  • Land and vegetation understanding - Our background in horticulture and land-based systems helps us interpret site conditions with real-world context.
  • Secure in-house processing - Project data is stored on company-controlled systems and processed internally where possible, rather than routinely sent to third-party cloud processors.
  • Professional spatial workflow - From field capture to final delivery, our work is structured to support councils, rural clients and design professionals.
  • Respectful data handling - AerisAg recognises that land information may involve privacy, cultural values and data sovereignty considerations.
